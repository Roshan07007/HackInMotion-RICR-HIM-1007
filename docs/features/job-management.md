# Job Management & Recommendations

HireMe offers a comprehensive suite of tools for finding and managing job opportunities.

## Job Discovery & Recommendations
- **Platform Aggregation:** The system fetches and normalizes job listings not only from our internal database but also from external platforms, providing a unified feed of opportunities.
- **AI Recommendations:** Based on your tailored preferences (Target Roles, Technologies, Experience Level) and past activity, the platform intelligently curates and recommends the most relevant jobs to your feed.

## Job Interaction (Apply & Save)
- **External & Internal Applications:** Jobs can either be applied to directly within the platform or externally. When opening an external application link, the system seamlessly redirects you while automatically marking the job as "Opened/Applied" in your history.
- **Save & Bookmark:** Users can easily bookmark jobs they are interested in to review later. Saved jobs are synchronized across devices.
- **Status Tracking:** The platform maintains a clear history of jobs you have saved and applied to, making it easy to track your job search progress.
